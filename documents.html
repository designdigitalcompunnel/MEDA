Record types

What you can find here.

Each record type answers a different question about the authority and its work.

  • Board meeting minutes

    Minutes record what the board discussed and any actions or decisions it took during a meeting.

  • Bylaws

    Bylaws describe how the board is organized and how it conducts its business.

  • Establishment agreement

    The founding agreement sets out how the City of Craig and Moffat County established MEDA.

  • Policies and procedures

    These records describe how the authority carries out and documents its work.

For an approved record or an accessible copy, contact MEDA.

    Using the archive

    How to find a public record.

    A few details can help you find the correct document, especially when several records have similar titles.

    1. 01

      Choose a type

      Start with minutes, governing documents, or policies and procedures.

    2. 02

      Identify the year

      For minutes, use the meeting year to narrow the list.

    3. 03

      Check the title

      Search for the name, such as bylaws or establishment agreement.

    4. 04

      Check the date

      Confirm the meeting date or revision date before opening a record.

    5. 05

      Open the PDF

      Read the record in your browser or save a copy for reference.

    6. 06

      Ask for help

      Contact MEDA for an official copy or a different format.

    Include the record title or meeting date when you contact MEDA.

    Reading the records

    What records tell you.

    Public records help residents trace board actions and understand the rules that guide the authority.

    • Meeting actions

      Minutes can show the topics discussed, motions, and recorded votes.

    • Board rules

      Bylaws describe officer roles, membership, and meeting procedures.

    • Founding purpose

      The establishment agreement describes why the City and County created MEDA.

    • Operations

      Policies and procedures describe how the authority carries out and records its work.
