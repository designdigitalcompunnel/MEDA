04 — About MEDA

What MEDA does.

The City of Craig and Moffat County formed MEDA to manage settlement funds tied to the closure of Craig Station and invest the returns in the local economy.

07 — Economic priorities

What we work toward.

MEDA focuses on the long-term needs of Craig and Moffat County as the local economy adapts to the coal transition.

Quality jobsSupport projects that create or retain jobs for local residents.
New investmentAttract capital for facilities, equipment, and infrastructure.
Stable tax baseHelp sustain the local tax base that supports public services.
Local oversightKeep decisions with a board appointed by local public bodies.
Invest settlement funds with the long-term needs of Craig and Moffat County in view.

What guides MEDALocal jobs, capital investment, and a stable tax base.

08 — Governance

Our board and local partners.

A five-member volunteer board governs MEDA. The Craig City Council, Moffat County Board of County Commissioners, and Moffat County School District RE-1 appoint its members.
